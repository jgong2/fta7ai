What SAA is

SAA — Supplier Audit Agent

SAA is FTA’s AI assistant for supplier audits. An auditor describes what they observed; SAA maps it to the relevant clause, lists the objective evidence to request, and suggests a grade (Major / Minor / OFI) for the auditor to confirm. We are building it standard by standard:

1 · ISO 9001:2015 §8.4 — firstControl of externally provided processes, products and services. Active now.
2 · IATF 16949 — nextAutomotive supplier control on top of ISO 9001 §8.4.
3 · ISO 13485 — thenPurchasing controls for medical devices (§7.4).
